Sign in with your own Google script

Google lets the shared sign-in serve only 100 people. This way goes through a small script in your own Google account instead, so there is no limit and nothing is shared with anyone. About three minutes, easiest on a computer.

  1. Make your copy. Open the script, press Overview (ⓘ) on the left, then Make a copy (⧉) at the top right.
  2. Make your copy. Open script.new and replace everything in Code.gs with this:
    Then open Project Settings (⚙), tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor, go back to the editor, and replace everything in appsscript.json with this:
    Press Save.
  3. Deploy it. Deploy → New deployment → ⚙ → Web app. Check it says Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then press Deploy and Authorize access.
  4. Allow it. Google says it hasn't verified this app — expected for your own script. Check the email it shows is yours, then Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow. If it names anyone else, stop.
  5. Paste its link. Copy the Web app URL — it ends in /exec.
